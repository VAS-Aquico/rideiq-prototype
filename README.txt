RideIQ Platform Prototype
=========================
Version 3 · 28 October 2026 · VAS Acquico

What this is
A clickable prototype of the RideIQ platform for client review. All figures,
operators and people are simulated sample data. Nothing is connected to a
live system.

How to view it
- On your computer: double-click index.html (opens in your web browser).
- On a web server: upload index.html to any web host or static hosting
  (for example an S3 bucket, Netlify, or your own server). No other files
  are needed. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online.

What to look at
- Landing page, then "Sign in to your portal" to choose a portal.
- "View as" (top bar) switches between NIPOST / CLRD, Operator,
  TAP finance and VAS admin.
- Theme button switches between light and dark.
